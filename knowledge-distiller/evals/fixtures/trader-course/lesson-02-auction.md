# Lesson 02: Auction Signal

Auction behavior between 09:20 and 09:25 can reveal whether attention is concentrating or fading. The signal is not the raw volume alone; it is the change in intent.

When indicative price strengthens while cancellation pressure falls, the crowd may be moving from testing to commitment. This is useful only when the broader structure supports continuation.

Bad use: buying every strong auction print. A strong print inside a weak structure often becomes exit liquidity.

Research seed: compare auction-strength delta with next-session return, controlling for liquidity, sector cycle, and previous-day limit-up status.
