# Lesson 01: Cycle and Expectation

Strong trades begin with a market structure that can support expectation. In a weak cycle, the bottom is hard to define. Do not treat every rebound as a new trend.

The practical rule is to ask whether the expected catalyst has a floor. If the floor is absent, reduce the trade to observation or a very small tactical attempt.

Example: when the leading sector has no follow-through after two sessions, auction strength alone is not enough. The auction may show emotion, but emotion without structure is fragile.

Boundary: this does not mean never trading weak cycles. It means weak-cycle trades need higher expected payoff, cleaner exits, and less confidence.

Evidence note: repeated lesson emphasis on cycle, floor, expectation, and fragile rebounds.
