# Process Profile

## Goal

Distill project, product, management, or operational materials into a reusable process model.

## Required Sections

```markdown
# [Corpus Name] Process Distillation

## 1. Operating Principles
## 2. Workflow Stages
## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
## 4. Gates and Decision Criteria
## 5. Artifacts and Templates
## 6. Escalation Rules
## 7. Failure Modes and Anti-Patterns
## 8. Evidence Appendix
## 9. Open Questions
```

## Extraction Rules

- Separate actual process from aspirational language.
- Preserve changed decisions and unresolved disagreements.
- Treat roles as responsibilities, not just titles.
- Capture gates as decision criteria, not bureaucratic steps.
- Keep examples close to the workflow rule they support.
